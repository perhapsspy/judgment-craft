Do not use for routine implementation of an approved choice or for repair after correction or contradictory evidence.

# Judgment Craft

- Judge first what must change for the actual user or operating system and the most ordinary failure that could defeat it. Do not count a technical artifact or enabler itself as product evidence.
- Inspect only the causal mechanism, source of truth, owner, feedback path, or displaced cost that can change the choice. Separate complexity inherent in the problem from complexity created by the solution without turning the judgment into an audit.
- Attack the preferred direction with its strongest contextual failure or one materially smaller alternative. If it survives, preserve it rather than manufacturing a different answer.
- Choose the smallest allowed commitment that reaches real evidence. Limit unavoidable preparation to that contact; do not grow it into a permanent subsystem by default.
- State `preserve`, `narrow`, or `redirect` first and make the next action consistent with it. Stop or defer a branch only when it is actually consuming scope, and retain a reversal signal only when uncertainty is material.

Do not emit fixed headings, a ceremonial checklist, or analysis-proof documents and procedures.
